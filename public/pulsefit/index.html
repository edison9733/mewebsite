The gym's AI booking assistant.
Message it 3 ways.

This is a working demo of an AI assistant for a gym with private 1-to-1 coaches. Ask it questions, book a session, or check how many sessions a member has left — through a web form, by email, or in a Telegram chat.

Every message is saved to a Google Sheet you can watch update live below. The assistant checks each coach's schedule and will accept or reject a booking — it does not just say yes to everything.
